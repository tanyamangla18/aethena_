Aethena prototype

A ride-hailing app with safety built in. Everything marked simulated is a demo. No real calls, payments, recordings or alerts are made.

  1. Home: tap Where to? or a saved place. Rides and Generalised (safety tools) sit under Explore.
  2. Book: pick a ride, confirm, watch the driver arrive, share the PIN and Start ride.
  3. Detour test: in Demo controls press Traffic diversion. No alarm is raised.
  4. Real deviation: press Unexplained deviation. Grace period, quiet check-in, the Are You Safe? call, then contacts are alerted and Evidence mode starts.
  5. Phone off or Battery 14% show escalation without a reply. Evidence queues offline and syncs on Reconnect.
  6. Evidence mode: Safety tab, or the record switch during a ride. Start, watch it back up, stop, then share from the vault.
  7. Fake call: Safety, Fake calls, 3 s, Schedule call.
  8. Pay and review, then Account, Driver dashboard for reconciliation.
